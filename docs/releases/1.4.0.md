# EBB 1.4.0: the map redesign

Every room in EBB has been rebuilt. Rooms were all one screen, and every one was a box; now a
room can be up to two screens by two, the camera follows you, and the places along the way are
built as places. A fifth biome opens a second road through the night.

**Download `EBB-Setup-1.4.0.exe` below and run it.** It installs for your user with no admin
rights. Run it over 1.3.0 and it updates that install in place. Saves, settings and replays carry
over, and a night you rested in 1.3.0 picks up where you left it.

## New

- **The Reef, a second road.** Past the Wreck the night forks: the Sunken Harbour, or the Reef
  outside Saltmere's sea wall. On the Reef the water runs in currents that carry you and
  everything else, and living coral cuts whatever the current drags across it. Four new
  enemies (the Coral Crab, the Reef Ray, the Polyp Swarm and the Reefwarden), a new boss (the
  Tidewall, which turns the current at its word), and a new echo: Hale the Mason, caged in the
  sea wall. Both roads meet again in the Drowned Streets.
- **Bigger rooms, and a camera that follows you.** Fights run from one screen to two by two,
  in corridors, L-shapes, rings, terraces and flooded rooms.
- **Fresh fights every night.** Half the fighting rooms are drawn by a generator from the
  night's seed, so no two nights have the same rooms. A daily or seeded night is still the same
  for everyone who plays it.
- **Places, not boxes.** Every merchant, altar, fountain, echo cage and boss arena in every
  biome is built for where it is: the merchant on his pier, the chapel up its steps, the Bell
  under its tower, the cage in the sea wall's breach. Marrow Rock is a place on the rock now,
  with each person at a spot made from what came home with them.
- **Ground with height.** Low ground floods first, the floor next, raised ground last, and the
  ground draws its edges, banks and walls. Each room is lit by its own lamps.

## Changed

- The Wreck's beach has a bank of raised rock to fight on. The old beach drowned too many
  nights when the Wreck dragged the tide in.
- The water no longer stutters a fight in a large room when the tide rises.
- Every room leads two ways on, and the boss arenas have a door for each road.

## Also

- The night's balance was measured again over the new rooms: 16.7 rooms a night on average for a
  fresh keeper played by the test bot, against 15.2 before.
- The daily board's par is slower (17.5 s a room, was 12.7): the rooms are larger.
- For builders: a room editor that knows the hub and the tide (`tools/edit_room.ps1`), a frame-time
  probe for the large rooms (`tools/perf.ps1`), and the 13-stage plan in `MAP_ROADMAP.md`.
