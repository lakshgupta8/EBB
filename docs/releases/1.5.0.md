# EBB 1.5.0: the sound of the night, and two keepers

EBB has been re-scored and re-sounded from the ground up. Every place has its own music that follows
the night, every enemy warns you in its own voice, and the sea, the streets and the drowned sound
like where they are. Also new: a second keeper can join you, to share a night or to duel.

**Download `EBB-Setup-1.5.0.exe` below and run it.** It installs for your user with no admin
rights. Run it over 1.4.0 and it updates that install in place. Saves, settings and replays carry
over. A settings file from before 1.5 keeps its volume: the one sound slider becomes the new
effects, ambience, interface and voice sliders.

## New

- **Two keepers.** On the title screen, choose **Two keepers**. One player hosts and the other joins by
  address, on the same network or over a VPN such as Tailscale (UDP port 24680). Both players need 1.5.0.
  - **Co-op:** share a night's fights and bosses.
  - **Duel:** a best-of-three arena fight on equal stats.
- **A score for every place.**
  - The Flats in a minor key, with the Wreck in two phases.
  - The Harbour as a work song in 6/8, the Chainmother's chains in it.
  - The Reef's harp following the current.
  - The Streets keeping time to the Bell's toll.
  - Maren's hum in the Hall's nave.

  The music follows the night: it builds into a fight, swells with the tide, changes with each boss
  phase and resolves on the kill. The rock gains an instrument for each keeper you bring home. The title
  and the endings have their own themes.
- **Every enemy warns in its own voice.** A windup is the first sound you hear, even from off screen,
  and it comes from where the enemy stands. Hits and deaths sound of what was struck: wood, stone,
  shell, coral, metal, wax. Each of the six weapons has its own set.
- **The world underfoot.** Footsteps change with the ground and the depth of the water: sand, stone,
  cobbles, planks, coral, the shallows and deep water. You hear the tide come in. On the Reef the current runs round your legs.
- **Places that breathe.** Each place's ambience shifts as the tide rises from dry to flooded, and
  its wrecks, wells, lamps, cranes, pools and an organ sound where they stand.
- **The drowned.** Saltmere's drowned breathe and groan as they wander. The Choir holds its note under
  everything it does, and you can hear it falter.
- **Voices and menus.** Each person on the rock babbles in a voice of their own. The menus sound of
  wood, brass and glass.
- **Pictures in the codex.** Each place you have reached shows a picture of itself.

## Changed

- **Sound settings:** the single sound slider is now six: master, music, effects, ambience,
  interface and voices.
- **A Mono switch** on the Sound page plays the same sound from both speakers.
- **Reverb per place:** each place has its own echo, small on the Flats and wide in the Deep Hall.
- **The mix is balanced:** effects no longer drown the music, every place plays at about the same
  loudness, and nothing distorts at full volume.
- **Download size:** the setup is now 78 MB (was 32 MB), almost all of it the new music and ambience.

## Known limits of two keepers

- The guest fights only, with base stats and the gaff. Shops, altars, tidemarks and the village are
  the host's.
- A lost connection ends the night. There is no reconnect.
